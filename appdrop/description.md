**Remember why you called.**

You call someone, they don't pick up, and by the time they ring back you've forgotten what it was about. Recal fixes exactly that, and nothing else.

### How it works
- **They don't answer:** a compact card asks *why you called*. A few words, one tap.
- **They call back:** your reason is on screen **before you answer**, over the incoming-call screen and during the call, even on the lock screen.
- **You talk:** once you've answered and the call ends, the reminder clears itself. Miss or reject the callback and it stays.
- Multiple reminders per person, and a fresh one every time a later call goes unanswered.

### Features
- Works on the lock screen, with a switch to keep reasons hidden until you unlock
- Optional **In-call Notes** (off by default): a small per-contact notepad during calls
- Add or tick off reminders by hand from the home screen
- Light, dark or system theme
- Notification fallback when the bubble can't appear

### Private by design
- **No internet permission.** Nothing can leave your phone.
- No account, no ads, no analytics, no cloud
- Everything is stored in a local database; backups are off; uninstall removes it all

### Good to know
- Android reports whether an outgoing call connected only after it ends, so the question appears a second or two after you hang up.
- If voicemail picks up, Android counts the call as answered. Add the reason with the + button.
- WhatsApp and other internet calls aren't visible to Recal.
- Needs *Phone & call log*, *Display over other apps* and *Notifications*. On Xiaomi, Samsung and OnePlus phones, allow background pop-ups too (Settings shows the steps).

Requires Android 8.0 or newer. Source and docs on GitHub.
